Use this format for longer updates such as accepted papers, grant announcements, software releases, workshops, or invited talks.

Suggested structure:

1. One-sentence summary of the update.
2. Brief context (venue, event, or project).
3. Link to paper, code, or event page when available.

Example placeholder:

- [YYYY.MM.DD] [Paper/Project/Event title]
- [1-2 sentence factual summary]
- [Link placeholder]
