We welcome inquiries from prospective PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and research collaborators.

## Prospective PhD Students

Please include the following in your initial email:

- A short statement of research interests
- CV
- Unofficial transcripts (if available)
- Links to publications, projects, or code repositories

## Postdoctoral Researchers

When contacting us, please share:

- CV
- Research statement
- Representative papers
- Availability and potential funding context

## Research Interns and Visiting Students

Openings vary by project cycle. Please send a concise overview of your background, technical skills, and preferred research direction.

## Collaborations

We are interested in collaborations with academic, clinical, and scientific partners where rigorous methodological work can be paired with meaningful application problems.

For all inquiries, see [Contact](/contact/).
