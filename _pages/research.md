Our research focuses on building robust, interpretable, and useful machine learning methods for scientific and high-impact domains.

## Research Areas

### Trustworthy and Uncertainty-Aware Learning

We study calibration, uncertainty estimation, and reliability in data-limited settings. This includes methods for confidence-aware prediction and robust deployment under distributional shift.

### Multimodal and Scientific Representation Learning

We develop representation learning methods for heterogeneous scientific and biomedical data, including settings with missing modalities and complex acquisition pipelines.

### Causal and Interpretable ML for Decision Support

We investigate causal inference and interpretable modeling for high-stakes applications, with emphasis on transparency, robustness, and actionable analysis.
